About Lake District Tweed

A piece of the Lake District, made to last.

Woven from the fleeces of sheep grazing the Lake District fells, our tweed carries the strength, character and beauty of this unique landscape.

Our designs begin with the Lancs & Lakes Guild of Spinners, Weavers and Dyers, and every cloth is woven in the North West of England.
